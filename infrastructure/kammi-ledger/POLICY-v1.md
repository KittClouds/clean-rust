# Policy and actor authority v1

The controlled KAMMI_POLICY_V1 vocabulary permits sealed scientific/execution
specifications, verified predecessor seals, exact actor grants and fenced GPU leases.
Forbidden conditions are truth-label contact and evaluation-panel exposure.
Unknown predicate keys fail closed. A policy check emits a receipt and confers no authority.

Grants bind actor, action, run, stage, policy hash and expiry. Credentials are hashed;
raw credentials are not journal payloads. API credentials authenticate callers;
grants and policy decisions authorize operations. Master credentials administer the registry.

AuthorizationIssued binds current spec identities, policy identity and evidence head.
Another actor/run/stage, policy replacement, spec replacement or expiry invalidates reuse.
Historical imported assertions cannot satisfy live prerequisites.
Acceptance fixture authorization is restricted to acceptance.* run identities.
New-flight authorization requires an accepted current LibraryAcceptanceV1.

Artifact type names describe bytes. They cannot introduce new authorization predicates.
Custody vocabulary changes require Library Lab implementation and qualification.
Every capability consumer rechecks current live predicates under the authority
lock. A new forbidden truth contact or expired required resource invalidates
consumption even when the policy hash, actor and nominal authorization expiry
still match. Remote returns are rechecked after output ingestion and immediately
before the verification event is journaled. Partial uploads remain in history;
they do not become accepted results.
