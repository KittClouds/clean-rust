# REDLINE R2: BM25F-anchored residual diagnostic

R2 tests whether Phoenix evidence can earn bounded update authority after the BM25F lexical feature is fixed as the score anchor. Candidate generation, tokenization, evidence production, and the top-100 literal candidate universe remain frozen.

The learner uses a pairwise weak-negative objective over query-grouped candidates. Negatives are recorded by provenance: the first six non-relevant candidates in Phoenix order, the first six non-relevant candidates in BM25F order, and their overlap. The BEIR qrels are positive-only, so every unjudged candidate remains a weak negative and the receipt is diagnostic only.

For a candidate with evidence vector `f`, the diagnostic score is:

```text
score(q, d) = f[0] + sum(residual_weight[i] * f[i])
```

Residual weights are signed and clipped to a per-model bound. Development selection starts from the zero-residual anchor and rejects configurations that exceed the top-10 regression guard. Positional features are excluded from the full residual arm; they remain a separate future experiment.

The authority census evaluates each non-positional feature independently over a small bounded grid. It reports the selected development weight, nDCG change against BM25F, top-10 loss, and whether the feature earned non-zero authority under the guard.

## Result

The R2 models selected the zero-residual fallback on both datasets. That is a successful safety result: the anchored learner did not publish a harmful model, and its serving-equivalent score is exactly the BM25F anchor.

| dataset | BM25F anchor nDCG@10 | R2 lexical | R2 full non-positional | anchor top-10 losses | R2 promotions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SciFact | 0.675714 | 0.675714 | 0.675714 | 2 | 6 |
| FiQA | 0.231880 | 0.231880 | 0.231880 | 35 | 104 |

The authority census found one meaningful bounded signal on FiQA development data: `rarest_matched_term` selected `+0.10` and improved development nDCG by about `+0.01` while staying inside the guard. The joint learner did not retain that signal on the held-out test split, so it is a feature lead, not a qualified ranker.

## Decision

Keep BM25F as the qualified lexical foundation. Do not promote the weak-negative residual models. The next defensible learning step is an authoritative or judged-negative contract, followed by a tightly scoped residual experiment around the FiQA rare-term signal. Positional residuals remain gated until that contract exists.
