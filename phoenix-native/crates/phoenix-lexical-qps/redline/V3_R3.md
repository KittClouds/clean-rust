# REDLINE R3: Judged-negative rarity authority

R3 was designed as a narrow microscope around the `rarest_matched_term` lead from R2. The scientific arms were predeclared:

```text
R0 = BM25F
R1 = BM25F + bounded rarest matched term
R2 = BM25F + bounded rarest matched term * coverage
R3 = BM25F + bounded rarity-distribution gap
```

No positional features, transport, joint learner, or candidate-generation change is permitted.

The first gate is the authority contract:

```text
only explicit score-0 qrels are authoritative negatives
unjudged documents remain observation-only
```

## Result

## Decision

The rare-term lead remains a diagnostic hypothesis only. To run the four-arm microscope, Phoenix needs a judged-negative source or a separately sealed human-judgment protocol. Until then, BM25F remains the qualified lexical floor and no residual feature earns update authority.
