# Phase 0 Attribute Fixture

Brynwyn is a tall cartographer with silver-black hair, a scar through her left eyebrow, and ink stains on both cuffs. She wears a green surveyor coat lined with brass buttons.

Her voice is low and precise. Her left hand shakes when she is tired, but her map script remains perfectly even.

Brynwyn carries a bone compass, three folded harbor charts, and a knife with a cracked amber hilt.

She trusts Aella, mistrusts Rowan, and refuses to enter any room without two exits.
