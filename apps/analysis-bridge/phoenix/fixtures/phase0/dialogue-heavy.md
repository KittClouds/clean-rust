# Phase 0 Dialogue Fixture

"Rowan, close the hatch," Aella said.

"If I close it, Brynwyn is outside," Rowan said.

"Brynwyn has the mirror key," Kai said. "She can open the north latch."

"Not if the latch is frozen."

Siofra laughed from the stairwell. "The latch is not frozen. Rowan is afraid of the dark."

"I am afraid of leaving people behind," Rowan said.

The hatch wheel turned before anyone answered.
