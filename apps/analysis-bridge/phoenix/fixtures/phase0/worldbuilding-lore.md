# Phase 0 Lore Fixture

The Meridian Compact began as a salt-road treaty between Aurora, Phaeris, and the river towns of Ivalen. Its first law forbade any house from owning both a bridge and a ferry.

The compact stores judgments in copper books. Each book is chained beneath the Hall of Tides, where clerks recite the River Oath at the turning of every season.

The Mirror Guild predates the compact. Guild mirrors are not glass; they are polished moon-iron set into cedar frames.

Only a sworn navigator may name a mirror on a public map.
