# Phase 0 Single Chapter

## Chapter 1: The Quiet Signal

Aella crossed the empty relay room before dawn. The blue repeater on the west wall clicked twice, then held its light steady.

Kai wrote the numbers in his field book. He did not speak until the third pulse arrived.

"It is not a storm," Aella said.

"Then someone is answering us," Kai said.

The message ended with the old coast code for safe passage.
