# Phase 0 Causal Fixture

The south pump failed because the intake grate was packed with river glass. When the pump failed, the lower cistern drained into the turbine room.

Because the turbine room flooded, the night beacon went dark. The dark beacon made the courier ship turn east instead of west.

The ship missed the harbor, so Phaeris never received the truce letter.

Without the letter, Phaeris ordered the bridge closed.
