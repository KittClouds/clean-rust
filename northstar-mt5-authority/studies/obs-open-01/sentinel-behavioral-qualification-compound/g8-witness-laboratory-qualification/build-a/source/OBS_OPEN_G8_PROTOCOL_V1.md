# OBS-OPEN G8 — Witness Laboratory Qualification

G8 qualifies witness-search infrastructure on synthetic and formally understood systems before any real 04A population content is read.

Search status, artifact provenance, and G7-authorized verdict are distinct types. Explorers have no scientific authority. Certificates carry primitive evidence and may carry derived claims for audit, but the verifier independently recomputes semantics. Every authoritative certificate is bound to G3–G7 roots, ThetaStar, its schema, the verifier contract, and the authority bundle.

The separator verifier consumes an independently qualified comparison-fiber certificate and a frozen comparison instance. It rejects illegal starts, illegal continuations, derived-claim drift, and cross-lineage replay. Lambda and correspondence constitutions are never searched for convenience.

Canonical-first witnesses use a deterministic fair finite-shell ranking over unbounded integer coordinates. Shrinking is deletion-only in this generation and terminates by strict token-length decrease; it earns no global minimality. Exact bounded silence requires an independently verified finite-domain coverage receipt. Finite-box minimality is scoped to that box.

Universal equivalence authority is qualified only for `FINITE_PRODUCT_INDUCTIVE_RELATION_V1`, a named proof calculus over finite deterministic total systems. It does not apply to 04A without a future proven reduction.

No real 04A history, trace, pair, distribution, or value-conditioned heuristic is read. No outcomes are read. No production 04A judge, quotient, state minimization, memory minimization, field-removability, prediction, economic, or trading authority is earned.
