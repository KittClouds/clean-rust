# OBS-OPEN-03A-P — Future Process Atlas Protocol and Derived-Outcome Firewall

Authority class: protocol freeze only.

This gate defines a neutral retrospective future-process measurement atlas without computing it on real sessions. It exists because the 257-session market paths were already read by DISC-02E, while the new `OUTCOME_REGISTRY_V1` has not yet been applied. It therefore never claims that the underlying `D_B` paths are unopened. The protected authority is the uncomputed derived outcome layer, target selection, and formal representation score.

## Population roles

- `D_A`: 154 sessions for OBS-OPEN-03A descriptive atlas and question generation.
- `D_B`: 103 sessions reserved for a future representation-information gate after its own target/probe protocol is frozen.
- `D_C`: the existing 69-session confirmation population, still `FROZEN_UNOPENED` and not decoded by this gate.

`D_A` and `D_B` are assigned using a salted SHA-256 rank over the 257 already-open discovery session IDs. No market or outcome value participates. The exact membership is sealed.

## Observation law

Every future outcome is a retrospective sidecar with explicit `anchor_known_at`, observation window, `outcome_known_at`, evaluability, coverage, and censoring state. It never becomes a field of the causal anchor. A future fact known at 10:47 remains unavailable to a 10:31 anchor even when both appear in one retrospective record.

Candidate anchors retain their observed upper/lower orientation. Range-freeze anchors have no direction. No future path is used to orient an anchor.

## 03A authority

03A may materialize the registry only on `D_A` and report complete descriptive distributions, censoring, tails, event-time survival, and scale/horizon views. It may not run formal tests, promote candidates, select a best range, access `D_B`, or access `D_C`.

## Later gates

A future 03B protocol may select a target using `D_A`, but must freeze the target, nuisance/design context, probe class, folds, score, effect-size floor, inference, and decision rule before computing any `D_B` registry value. Its baseline must be `Y ~ C_t`, not a globally unconditional distribution.

`D_C` receives no automatic role. It remains unopened unless a later prospectively frozen gate earns explicit authorization.

Maximum authority from this seal: `OBS_OPEN_03AP_FUTURE_PROCESS_PROTOCOL_V1`. No empirical, predictive, confirmatory, economic, or trading authority is granted.
