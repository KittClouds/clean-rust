# REQUAL1-SREPLACE-SINGLES: fresh S-replacement singleton requalification

This engineering-only identity closes the semantic gap between the fresh
baseline-relative singleton receipts and the historical LR1 replacement
operation. It starts from each current fresh PAR8-reference `S` state, replaces
one raw group at a time with a current palette candidate, and replays the
exact sequential-f32 learner.

The eight contexts, `S` mappings, and `V` comparisons come only from the
fresh REQUAL1 PAR8 reference receipt. Historical LR1 receipts, selector
counts, and pair outcomes are not inputs. This identity emits singleton
records only; it does not shortlist, replay pairs, or open science.

Each context shard is immutable and independently bound. A final execution
receipt is emitted only after all 3,696 current candidates have been checked.
