# Q10 PI/DA supervisory review

This leg separates target-blind interaction mapping from target-conditioned
distributed readout repair. Luna xhigh agents own separate new PI1 and DA1
directories. No sealed predecessor is edited. Engineering samples only.

## Corrections carried forward

PF4's raw bundle receipts contain one positive shared triple and four positive
prefix combinations, not four positive triples. The older RESULT and STATUS
aggregation is inaccurate on this count. This note does not rewrite them.
Observed two/three-coordinate components were sampled as pairs/triples; their
sizes cannot establish that full structural neighborhoods are small.

JBR2 verifies a bounded enumeration, not an iterative accept-and-replay repair
policy. Its directions were fixed per block and its grid omitted zero. A best
candidate could therefore be worse than taking no action. DA1 must include
zero and independently verify its fully combined committed endpoint.

JBR3's midpoint selector took minima across whole rows, including positions
before the manipulated coordinates. Zero scores do not demonstrate useful
downstream sensitivity. PI1 measures actual joint interaction instead.

## PI1 acceptance

- Fixed pairs and signed prefixes are selected without a target/error input.
- Write and hash the interaction map before evaluating the target overlay.
- Normalize by a target-independent scale; retain raw norms and sparse values.
- Preserve original sequential order, duplicate coordinate occurrences, and
  signed-zero semantics. Full-oracle parity is a qualification requirement.
- Disjoint controls must have no numeric interaction; zero shared interaction
  remains an admissible result, not a reason to resample.
- Overlay reports interaction alignment and actual joint effects separately.
  Neither implies a globally valid repair endpoint.

## DA1 acceptance

- Coordinate supports are disjoint across ALL readout rows, not just initially
  mismatched rows or nonzero isolated effects.
- Greedy maximal supports are not maximum-coverage solutions. Freeze ordering
  before outcomes and retain all declared orderings in the report.
- Include zero and signed 1/2/4/8/16-ULP alternatives relative to the initial
  committed alternate. Keep final support, boundary and reserve legal.
- With disjoint supports, mismatch counts and squared residuals separate by
  coordinate. Selecting per-coordinate lexicographic minima is exact for the
  chosen set/vocabulary, not globally optimal over all coordinate sets.
- Bitwise verify the assembled readout against full replay. Floating f64
  recombination is not a replacement for this check.
- Mismatch-first optimization may increase L2. Record tradeoffs and Pareto
  improvement explicitly. Do not label every L2 decline an improvement.
- Record uncovered error, coverage and nested fixed-order coalition curves.
- Report final committed geometric drift and residual direction. The legacy
  64-move gate is diagnostic; it must not silently cap this distributed assay.

Tests, manifests, anatomy and runtime identity are reviewed before engineering
execution. Failures of scientific matching are valid diagnostic outcomes;
implementation/parity failures invalidate their affected qualification.
