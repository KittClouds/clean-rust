## What this means

- **The boundary for FF:** on this operation, the frozen 230M pair observer's *ranking* transfers across renderers (C-G0) but its *score* does not; per-world relative normalisation recovers most renderers and not S9. A replacement source is judged on the same two things: residual ranking inside the edge-carrying type pairs, and whether its score transports.
- **T1's marginal value at a controllable operating point is small** — about five points of non-edge pruning on top of a type table that already prunes 76.3% at zero edge loss. Whether that justifies carrying the substrate depends on a real workload's cost of a kept pair, which BANK cannot supply.
- **If the derating candidate is pursued,** it is a design decision for the runtime contract (a fixed safety factor between the DEV budget and the stated target, on relative type-pair-percentile scores), to be frozen and then confirmed once on the fresh split with bounds stated in advance; nothing more should be tuned on TEST.
- **Caution carried from C-G0:** T0's exact transfer is a property of BANK's synthetic schema (twelve type pairs with zero TRAIN edges). It is evidence inside BANK, not that real graphs have equally hard type exclusions.

## Limits

Development-grade throughout: TEST helped select the operation and shaped the transform family. Four transforms were tried (a small, preregistered family), plus one exploratory derating table. Percentile scales are discrete, so thresholds are coarse (visible in the identical rows at very small budgets).
No per-renderer guarantee was required or achieved. BANK graph targets are synthetic. The fresh sealed split (request drafted in `CONFIRM-GRAPH-SPLIT-REQUEST.md`, not sent) was not touched.
