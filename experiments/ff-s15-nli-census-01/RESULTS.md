# NLI-UNKNOWN oracle-headroom census — results

**Outcome under the preregistered rule: the NLI route is not earned, and it is dead at its ceiling.** A *perfect* NLI veto would lift ASK recall at precision 0.5 by only 6.7% (bar: 25%), so no NLI head could clear the bar.
CAL half of DEV only, no policy, no training, no HOLD, no TEST. Generated by `tools/make_results.py` from `results/census.json` (plan: `PLAN.md`, written before anything was computed).
The NLI head outputs were rebuilt from cached features and reproduce the sealed Rung 0 NLI predictions on all 24,000 DEV rows for every surface.

## The ceiling comes first: what a perfect NLI could do

BANK's NLI label is not specific to ASK. On CAL (11890 rows, 580 truth-ASK):

| truth decision | ENTAILED | CONTRADICTED | UNKNOWN | no NLI label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACT | 2254 | 0 | 2319 | 958 |
| ASK | 0 | 0 | 482 | 98 |
| ABSTAIN | 0 | 809 | 4004 | 966 |

`NLI == UNKNOWN` holds for 83% of ASK rows, but also 42% of ACT rows and 69% of ABSTAIN rows. A perfect NLI head that flagged exactly the UNKNOWN rows would be right 7.1% of the time
for ASK, against a base rate of 4.9% — a lift of about 1.5×. Its only real power is as a **veto**: an ENTAILED or CONTRADICTED row is never ASK (0% of them are), so a perfect NLI could remove false positives of `P(ASK)`.
But `P(ASK)`'s false positives are overwhelmingly UNKNOWN-labelled rows, so the veto removes few of them. Of its 105 false positives at the usable operating point, 84 are labelled UNKNOWN, 13 carry no NLI label and only 8 are
ENTAILED or CONTRADICTED (measured after the ceiling was read, to explain it). And 87 of the 105 are ABSTAIN rows (18 are ACT): the head's confusion is between ASK and ABSTAIN — two kinds of missing information — which NLI cannot tell apart.

| | recall at precision ≥ 0.5 | headroom over `P(ASK)` alone |
|---|---|---|
| existing `P(ASK)` alone (primary surface) | 18.1% | — |
| with a **perfect** NLI veto (unlabelled rows pass through) | 19.3% | **6.7%** |
| with a perfect NLI, strict (UNKNOWN only) | 16.7% | -7.6% |

## The actual NLI head (primary pairing: `P(ASK)` on `middle_plus_final` with `P(UNKNOWN)` of the NLI reference head on `layer_m4_final`)

| | rows | true ASK | false positives | precision | recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: existing `P(ASK)`, usable set (precision ≥ 0.5) | 210 | 105 | 105 | 50.0% | 18.1% |
| N: NLI-UNKNOWN, top-210 by `P(UNKNOWN)` (no threshold reaches 0.5) | 210 | 18 | 192 | 8.6% | 3.1% |
| A ∩ N | 10 | 8 | 2 | 80.0% | 1.4% |
| A ∪ N | 410 | 115 | 295 | 28.0% | 19.8% |

- **ASK misses recovered by NLI:** 10 of 475 (2.1%). **Oracle union headroom:** 9.5% (bar 25%); realizable union precision 28.0%, below the 0.5 the rule requires.
- **ASK false positives shared by NLI:** 2 of 105 (1.9%). NLI does not share `P(ASK)`'s mistakes — because it hardly relates to ASK at all: its standalone AP for ASK is 0.054–0.072
  (prevalence 4.9%), essentially chance. Requiring it would discard 97 of `A`'s 105 true positives to remove 103 of its 105 false positives: a near-random filter.
- **Veto/conjunction search** (`P(ASK) ≥ a AND P(UNKNOWN) ≥ b`, about 3,800 rules, in-sample on CAL): best headroom 0.0% (b = 0; b = 0 is `A` itself). Noise band from shuffled `P(UNKNOWN)`: median 0.0%, 95th percentile 0.0%, max 4.8%.
- The lift table (deciles of `P(ASK)` by terciles of `P(UNKNOWN)`) shows no consistent trend in `P(UNKNOWN)` within any `P(ASK)` decile; it is in `results/census.json`.

## Verdict

**V (veto):** not earned — conjunction headroom 0.0% against a 25% bar, and the perfect-NLI ceiling is 6.7%, so no head could clear it. **U (union):** not earned — oracle union headroom 9.5%, realizable union precision 28.0%.
Route dead. No experiment is earned from this census.

## What this means

- **"Different task ≠ useful evidence."** The NLI head is genuinely independent of `P(ASK)` — it shares almost none of its false positives — but only because it carries almost no information about ASK. Independence is worth something only if the second signal is informative.
- **BANK's NLI capability is not the missing-fact detector ASK needs.** UNKNOWN there means "the hypothesis is not entailed", which is equally true of most ACT and ABSTAIN rows. ASK is narrower: a fact the *goal's plan requires* is absent (in TRAIN, every ASK row has exactly one
  missing, requestable fact). That is goal-conditioned, and the census says the NLI label as defined does not carry it, even when perfect.
- **For the representation ladder (a hypothesis, not tested here):** the capability that would matter is closer to "is every fact this goal needs present?" — a goal-conditioned evidence-completeness check — than to NLI or NER as BANK defines them. Whether such a head can be trained from BANK's
  evidence and missing-fact labels is a question for Lexi's ladder; nothing in C-rung work has tested it. Until something does, the cheap tier's ASK ceiling stays about 50% precision at 15–20% recall (ASK rung), which costs the acting tier nothing.

## Limits

CAL only and in-sample: every headroom number here is an *upper bound* (that is the point of a census), and the conjunction search is optimistic by construction — which makes the null result stronger, not weaker. About 16% of rows carry no NLI label; the ceiling lets them pass through
(the strict variant is worse). One backbone, one seed, synthetic BANK. HOLD was not used and remains the C1/ASK holdout; the fresh sealed split request stays queued and unsent.
