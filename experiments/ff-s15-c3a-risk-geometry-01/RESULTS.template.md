## What this means

- C1's `min(P_decision, P_action)` is close to the best ordering these vectors offer in the operating range. The controller was not leaving a cheap, zero-training risk signal unused.
- A different scalar score would only help where the harm target is loose (about 10% and up). That is the loose end of the frontier, where harm is already near the edge of what the C2a write-up called C1's useful range (about α = 0.10).
- So the remaining lever on this controller is not a smarter score over the same vectors. C3b is not earned, and the census cannot say anything about whether *different information* would help.

## Limits

CAL only; thresholds are chosen in-sample for every score alike, so every count is optimistic, but the comparison between scores is fair. Row-level bootstrap (renderer-paired rows are treated as independent, a slight optimism for every score alike). Thirteen scores were compared, guarded by the 1st percentile rather than a formal correction.
Development evidence; HOLD was not used and stays the C1/ASK holdout.
