# REQUAL1-DOMAIN-R2: current-lineage measurement substrate

This identity rebuilds the premeasurement substrate below the broken historical
PF5/LR1 ancestry. It is engineering-only and does not replay singleton or pair
interventions.

The builder binds a fresh root manifest over the current source, data, runtime,
and configuration inputs; copies that manifest into two independent closure
twins; reconstructs the eight intended `seed9731` contexts from current bytes;
rederives current topology and raw groups; validates the current GP1 candidate
mappings against reconstructed endpoint bytes; and derives a new ordered
singleton domain from the current candidate inventory.

Historical contracts and receipts are documentation and candidate inputs only.
Their parent hashes, scores, and singleton/pair results are never treated as
fresh measurements. The builder stops before singleton smoke or materialization.

The domain gate requires byte-identical root manifests and identical derived
state, topology, group, candidate, and domain hashes across both closure twins.
Any reconstruction or candidate-byte mismatch is recorded as a closed failure.

No scientific seeds, behavioral probes, DH08B authorization, singleton replay,
or pair replay are permitted by this identity.
