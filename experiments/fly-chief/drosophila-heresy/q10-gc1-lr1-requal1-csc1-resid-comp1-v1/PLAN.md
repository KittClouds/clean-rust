# Q10-RESID-COMP1

Read-only compatibility audit restricted to the 33 residual rows that RESID1-R1
found individually targetable in at least one valid order-1 through order-3
state. The denominator for pairwise co-attainment is therefore fixed at
`C(33,2) = 528`, not all 7,503 pairs among the 123 residual rows.

The audit reports the targetable-row compatibility graph, connected components,
node degrees, class-stratified edge counts, observed simultaneous target-set
sizes, and the maximum observed targetable subset by interaction order.

It performs no replay and makes no claim that pairwise compatibility extends to
all 33 rows simultaneously. Engineering evidence only; order 4, GC2, AG1,
behavior, and scientific promotion remain closed.
