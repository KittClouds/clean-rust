# Q10-CSC1-ROUTE1: two-stage validity and utility router audit

ROUTE1 consumes only the fresh current-lineage UPAIR1 receipts. It performs no
replay, no fitting, and no outcome-conditioned domain selection. Each unordered
pair receipt becomes two directed source-to-partner observations.

The primary target is the first partner that produces an actually final-valid
pair that is strictly better than fresh V. Four frozen orderings are compared:

* deterministic random hash order;
* partner singleton readout score only;
* predicted structural geometry order;
* two-stage structural validity first, then partner singleton readout score.

The two-stage ordering tests the proposed division of labor directly:
structural geometry routes candidates toward admissibility, and singleton
readout ranks utility inside the admissible tier. Outcome labels are read only
from UPAIR1 receipts after all orderings are frozen.

This is a descriptive engineering audit. It does not establish a biological
routing mechanism and does not open global assembly, GC2, AG1, behavior, or
scientific seeds.
