# REQUAL1-SREPLACE-CANDIDATES: complete pair-eligible candidate library

This engineering-only identity derives the complete pair-eligible singleton
library from the fresh S-replacement singleton receipts. It performs no
replay and imports no historical LR1/SALL1 selector or count.

A candidate is eligible for the later pair-domain screen when it is a
nonidentity replacement and either passes the final geometry gates or is
strictly better than the fresh `V` comparator on the complete global score.
Valid candidates are retained at every score level because a locally worse
valid move may be a useful geometry complement. Invalid readout-improving
candidates are retained for the same cancellation reason.

This identity is a library derivation only. It does not claim pair
feasibility, global feasibility, or scientific relevance.
