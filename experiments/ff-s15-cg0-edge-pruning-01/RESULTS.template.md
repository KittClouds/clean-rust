## What this means

- **T1 earns advancement under the gate as written**, and the residual it removes — a further {resid_gain1} to {resid_gain2} points of non-edges beyond T0 at 1–2% budgets — is real, far outside the noise band and positive on every renderer.
- **The gate does not say T1 is a robust runtime tier.** On the held renderers the gain is under 10 points, S9 is weak, and a threshold fitted on DEV loses several times the intended fraction of edges. C-G1 must treat **calibration under renderer shift** as its first problem, and choose a threshold policy that keeps the edge-loss guarantee, not just the ranking.
- **T0 is a real tier, not a baseline column.** It is deterministic, exact across renderers, needs no model, and already handles about three quarters of the non-edges. Any runtime should have it, whatever T1 does.
- The fair FF question — can a token or from-scratch pair encoder replace the 230M for the residual? — is now well posed: the target is the residual frontier inside three type pairs, and T1's own numbers there are the bar to match.
