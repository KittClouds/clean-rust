# C-G0 results — residual edge pruning: does the frozen 230M pair observer extend a type-pair table?

**Preregistered gate: PASSED — T1 advances. But the pass is narrower and stranger than the headline, and it comes with a calibration problem that decides what C-G1 has to be.**
Pooled TEST gain over the type-pair table is **+11.9 points at ε = 1% and +14.5 at ε = 2%** (bar: 10 at both), positive on S7, S8 and S9 individually, and far above the shuffled-score noise band (0.25% and 0.48%).
Development-grade characterisation only: TEST and S7/S8/S9 helped select edge existence as the surviving operation, so none of these TEST numbers is independent validation. Generated by `tools/make_results.py` from `results/cg0-census.json`; plan: `PLAN.md` (fixed before anything was computed).

## Two populations, kept apart

**Sampled-pair capability (Lexi's result).** Balanced positives and an equal number of random non-edges per world. The frozen head reproduces her per-split AUC on all nine splits (worst difference 2.1e-09), so the cached-projection implementation used here is the head as delivered.
Her headline stands as she reported it: AUC 0.982 on TEST, 0.955 on held renderers, against her identifier control at 0.889 and 0.894.

**Full-pair pruning utility (this census).** Every ordered pair of typed entities in each world, at natural balance: **10.1% of pairs are edges** (3,188,518 TEST pairs, 320,765 edges). Nothing below is computed from her sample, and her AUC and these frontiers should not be read as the same quantity.

## T0 already does most of the pruning, for free

Only **3 of the 15** type pairs in TRAIN ever carry an edge — OBJECT→LOCATION (29.2%), AGENT→LOCATION (29.4%) and LOCATION→LOCATION (35.4%). The other twelve have **zero edges in 5,359,133 TRAIN pairs**. T0 prunes them all at no edge loss:
**76.3% of TEST non-edges** (77.0% on the held renderers), and T0's frontier is flat, since nothing inside the three edge-carrying type pairs can be pruned by type alone. T0 is a rule the runtime can already express, needs no model, and transfers exactly (edge loss 0.00% on every split).
T1's real job is only the residual: the 24% of non-edges that share a type pair with edges.

## The calibration problem: T1's ranking transfers, its threshold does not

Report-only in the plan, and it decides C-G1. Thresholds fitted on DEV at ε = 1% and applied unchanged:

| | edge loss achieved | non-edges pruned |
|---|---|---|
| T0 alone (any split) | 0.00% | 76.3% |
| T0+T1, DEV fit | 1.00% | 96.1% |
| … applied to pooled TEST | **6.65%** | 95.0% |
| … applied to held S7/S8/S9 | **12.89%** | 92.7% |
| … applied to S9 | **17.14%** | 88.6% |

A 1% budget fitted on DEV becomes **6.65% lost edges on TEST and 17.14% on S9**. The cause is visible in the scores (post hoc): the first percentile of edge scores is -0.70 on DEV but -2.45 to -5.11 across the TEST renderers, so DEV edges are markedly easier than TEST edges even in-family, and the DEV-fitted cut silently sacrifices edges everywhere.

Even at a 0.02% DEV budget the achieved loss is 1.5% pooled and 2.7% on the held renderers, still above the 1% intended, while T0+T1 still prunes 90% / 87% against T0's 76.3%.

## Confidence geometry: the useful lane is confident NO_EDGE

As predicted. Edge scores sit around a median of 1.58 (5th to 95th percentile -1.06 to 4.78); non-edge scores have a median of -22.3 and a very long negative tail (5th percentile -76.7): easy non-edges are rejected with extreme confidence, and the overlap with edges is a narrow band near zero.
Inside the three edge-carrying type pairs T1's AUC is **0.938 pooled and 0.861 on the held renderers** (T0's is 0.5 by construction there). The pooled full-pair AUC is 0.983, much of which is type-pair separation.

## What this means

- **T1 earns advancement under the gate as written**, and the residual it removes — a further 11.9 to 14.5 points of non-edges beyond T0 at 1–2% budgets — is real, far outside the noise band and positive on every renderer.
- **The gate does not say T1 is a robust runtime tier.** On the held renderers the gain is under 10 points, S9 is weak, and a threshold fitted on DEV loses several times the intended fraction of edges. C-G1 must treat **calibration under renderer shift** as its first problem, and choose a threshold policy that keeps the edge-loss guarantee, not just the ranking.
- **T0 is a real tier, not a baseline column.** It is deterministic, exact across renderers, needs no model, and already handles about three quarters of the non-edges. Any runtime should have it, whatever T1 does.
- The fair FF question — can a token or from-scratch pair encoder replace the 230M for the residual? — is now well posed: the target is the residual frontier inside three type pairs, and T1's own numbers there are the bar to match.

## Limits

TEST and the held renderers helped select this operation, so every TEST figure is development-grade; DEV is easier than TEST, so DEV-fitted numbers are optimistic. Matched frontiers choose parameters on the evaluated split for every arm alike (optimistic for all, fair between arms).
Types come from the entity-id prefix (given by the observation, not inferred from text); a real deployment needs a typer. BANK graph targets are synthetic; this is not a natural-language graph claim.
