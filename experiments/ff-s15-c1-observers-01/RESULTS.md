## What was run

The four Rung 0 carry-forward surfaces, each with its saved `decision` and `action_type` linear heads, rebuilt on DEV from the cached
features (no language model, no GPU). The rebuilt top labels match the sealed Rung 0 predictions on all 24,000 DEV rows, for every
surface and both heads, with confidences within 1e-5. Probabilities were quantized once to integer ppm and stored as prerecorded
C0 `DecisionVector`s bound to real `ObserverBundle`s that carry the Rung 0 hashes.

Thresholds for three rules (abstain, ask, act) were fitted on the calibration half (11,890 rows) at five precision targets α. The
HOLD half (12110 rows, no world or graph shared with CAL) was then run through the **real C0 runtime**: 242200 runs
across 20 policies. A numpy re-implementation of the rules agreed with the runtime on every row, and 500 rows per policy
re-run byte-identical.

## Reading it

**Confidence carries real information, and the cap transfers.** On the primary surface the controller executed 722 actions
and 37 were harmful (H = 5.1%); the no-threshold argmax baseline is wrong 38.5% of the time it acts. Shuffling the
confidences among the same candidates gives a median H of 38.2% (5th percentile 35.6%), so the confidence ordering is
doing the work. The fitted threshold held out of sample: H = 5.1% on HOLD against 3.6% on CAL, target 5%. That is the
black-box premise in miniature — a small typed head's confidence, cut at an integer threshold by a deterministic rule, buys a
harm rate the head does not have on its own.

**But the cheap tier does very little of the useful work, and that is what failed.** M2 fails on every surface. At the primary point
the controller resolves 30.2% of HOLD rows, yet most of that is abstention (2933 abstentions, 2783 of them on truth-ABSTAIN rows, so 94.9% precise, covering 48% of all truth-ABSTAIN rows). It executes a correct action on only 5.7% of rows (685 rows) — 12.2% of the rows whose truth is ACT — and
sends 86% of ACT rows to `USE_LARGER_MODEL`. Loosening α buys more execution only by paying for it in harm (frontier below).

**ASK is not handled at all.** No threshold reached even the loosest precision target for the ASK rule, so it was omitted at every α and
on every surface: of 584 truth-ASK rows on the primary surface, 555 were left unresolved and 6 were *executed* (harmful).
This is the Rung 0 finding (low ASK recall) arriving intact at the controller: the fast tier cannot ask for help when it should.

**The cap is not uniform across families.** At the primary point H is under 5% on six of the nine surface families but 9.7%-12.0% on S3, S4 and S5
(35-75 executed rows each, so noisy, but the direction is consistent with the permutation result and worth a look before any threshold is trusted).

## Sensitivity surfaces

`final_plus_mean` passes M1, M3, M4 and fails only M2. `layer_m4_final` fails M1 narrowly — its harm on HOLD (1.1%) was *lower* than on CAL (3.3%),
so the cap held, but the preregistered M1 is two-sided and the 2.1% gap exceeds 2 points; the criterion was not changed after the fact.
`full_mean` fits no ACT rule at α = 0.05 (nothing executed), consistent with its Rung 0 role as an ABSTAIN specialist rather than a router.

## Limits

- **DEV only.** TEST has been opened twice, so it cannot support a confirmatory claim, and DEV may have informed Rung 0's surface choice. The result is a development
  signal. A confirmatory C1 needs a **fresh sealed split** (new BANK seeds, truth withheld until predictions and thresholds are sealed); that has not been built.
- **BANK is synthetic**, one seed, one linear head architecture, one backbone, raw softmax with no calibrator. Authority is not exercised (BANK has no authority ground
  truth); C0's tests cover that stage.
- The M2 bar (10% correct-executed) and the M1 two-sided width are judgment calls fixed in advance; the numbers above let a reader apply a different bar.
- The runtime handled real vectors without error, but the cost units are C0's placeholders and say nothing about real compute.

## What this suggests for the next rung

The mechanism works: a deterministic controller can turn real observer confidence into a bounded harm rate and every decision is a replayable receipt.
What is missing is coverage of the *acting* decisions and any handling of ASK. Two directions follow from the data, neither built here:
observer coordination (C2 — do two surfaces agreeing raise the safely executable share above 12.2% of ACT rows?), and an ASK-capable observer or an explicit ask-by-default rule
for the escalation tier. Both need the fresh sealed split before their result can count as more than development evidence.
