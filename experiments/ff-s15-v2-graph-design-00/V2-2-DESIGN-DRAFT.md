# V2-2 — graph interface on BANK-v2: design draft (design only; nothing is run)

**Status:** DRAFT. This document reads **only the BANK-v2 constitution** (`ff-s15-bank-02/BANK-V2-FREEZE.md`). It touches no BANK-v2 record, label or DEV row, and V2-2 does not start until V2-0 has completed and its cue audit is frozen; otherwise the graph architecture could be shaped around shortcuts the instrument characterization exists to find first. It is confirmatory-lane work when it runs (frozen constitution, score once). C-G1b stays isolated and BANK-v2 is not its confirmation split.

## The question

> Which BANK-v2 decisions become deterministic graph operations given perfect materialization, and which still require learned perception or evidence adjudication?

## What X0 and X1 carry in

1. A graph controller over canonical facts is, in effect, BANK's own algebra: on an oracle graph the readout is exact, so the upper bound is a **consistency check of the instrument**, not a finding.
2. Schema-slot completeness is what makes ASK a graph deficiency; BANK-v2 makes this formal (obligations, alternatives, repairs), so the readout is now derivable, not a coin flip.
3. **X1:** arbitrating uncertain propositions as graph edges did not beat a *calibrated weighted-sum* score merger, and a merger cannot supply a fact no producer proposes. So: (a) every V2-2 comparison includes the calibrated flat merger as the baseline to beat, not max or mean; (b) any claimed benefit of structure must name the information the scores lack; (c) producers must actually cover the relation types the decisions need before arbitration is judged; (d) coverage levels are chosen after checking what is reachable.

| **V2-2d** | selective authority | given calibrated confidence on producers, when should the machine EXECUTE, ASK, ESCALATE or DECLINE, and does the graph's deficiency structure give a better stopping rule than a confidence threshold? | C0/C1-style matched-harm frontier; matched cost |

## A hypothesis map to test (not a result)

| decision family | expected to be a deterministic graph readout | expected to need perception or adjudication |
|---|---|---|
| conflicting supported pair (step 1) | yes, given the supported facts | recognizing the claim and its confidence in text |
| unknown entity / target, out of scope (1a) | yes, given introduced flags and the action closure | deciding that a mention is unbound |
| alias ambiguity (1a) | yes, given the alias table | building the alias table from text |
| unresolved obligations, requestability (2) | yes, given facts and the policy | reading the information policy declared in prose |
| goal satisfied, legal set, plan, ties (3 to 9) | yes: bounded search | none beyond fact extraction |
| escalation rules and permissions (6 to 8) | yes | reading the rules declared in prose |

The informative cells are the right-hand column: they are where learned perception is irreplaceable, and where V2-2c measures how much a graph layer adds.

## Fixed before V2-2 runs (to be written into its own constitution)

The exact candidate universe per slot; the producer ABI (extended with the declared policy and report channels); the merger baselines (max, mean, calibrated weighted sum); the graph mechanisms and their ablations; the coverage levels, after a reachability check on TRAIN; strata (reason, scope, slot, cardinality, renderer, intervention family); the use of the paired panels for attribution; and the rule that nothing is tuned on DEV.
