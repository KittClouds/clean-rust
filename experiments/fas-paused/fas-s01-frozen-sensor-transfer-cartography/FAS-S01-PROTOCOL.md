> **New information is not the same as a new representation.**
>
> The LFM backbone remains frozen: `Δθ_LFM = 0`.

## Question and starting observation

FAS-00 terminated with `SENSOR_FAIL_NO_SIGNAL` because its fixed final-layer
global-mean representation reached 0.5654062449331388 balanced accuracy on
the held-out context-term target slice, below the sealed 0.60 gate. The same
frozen sensor passed observation state, context identity, entity identity,
query relation, exact target, and held-out entity-term transfer. QUERY_ONLY and
cheap metadata controls stayed below their leakage ceilings.

FAS-S01 asks why target transfer differs between novel context and entity
terms. It is a diagnostic project. Its measurements cannot repair FAS-00,
change the FAS-00 threshold, or authorize FAS-00 online mechanisms.

## Hypotheses, not conclusions

| ID | Possible mechanism | Distinguishing observation |
|---|---|---|
| A | Global mean pooling dilutes context-bearing positions | Context transfer strengthens in a prospectively fixed span or position view while mean-full remains weak. |
| B | Lexical identity survives but context-to-target binding does not transfer linearly | Novel context identity is decodable, yet target margins collapse when context lexemes change alone. |
| C | Surrounding templates couple to context identity | Context contrast changes systematically across fixed paraphrases under identical latent rules. |
| D | Context information is present but inaccessible to a linear readout | A separately authorized nonlinear diagnostic shows signal after linear failure; it does not revise FAS-00. |
| E | Novel contexts are geometrically aliased | Distinct context substitutions occupy overlapping fixed-view neighborhoods after controlled matching. |
| F | The held-out context split is anomalous | A fresh, preregistered set of held-out context terms shows heterogeneous transfer, with all terms reported. |

No hypothesis is accepted from one retrospective geometry statistic. FAS-00's
revealed qualification split is exploratory for S01 and cannot serve as a new
confirmatory evaluation set.

## Phase S01-1: read-only global-feature cartography

After separate authorization, use only the existing FAS-00 FULL and QUERY_ONLY
global-mean vectors, Phase 1 v03 qualification metadata, and Phase 3 fixed
probe artifacts. The `read-only-cartography-contract-v01.json` fixes the
partitions, descriptive calculations, support reporting, and output namespace.

The existing cache does **not** contain `h(context term)` in isolation,
token-local hidden states, span means, last-token vectors, or any other new
readout surface. S01-1 may quantify the geometry of existing global vectors
and fixed Phase 3 margins; it cannot make claims about absent representations.

## Phase S01-2: controlled counterfactual corpus and extraction

This is reserved and unauthorized. A future versioned contract must first
freeze a new exact-world generator, invented-term inventory, seeds, quartet
construction, templates, tokenizer-span alignment, input identities, frozen
view definitions, and split. Each quartet will hold latent rule and target
fixed while varying context lexeme, entity lexeme, and an independent
paraphrase factor. All substitutions and failures must be reported.

Only after the corpus and extraction contract are sealed may a separate user
authorization permit loading the pinned LFM. Extraction remains single-row,
exact-length, no-padding, with an immutable backbone and a new S01-only cache.
Candidate readout surfaces for that future contract are final-layer mean-full,
last model-visible position, a designated first position, fixed context span,
fixed entity span, fixed relation span, and fixed-span concatenation. The
contract must define these exactly before extraction; this protocol does not
authorize trying them.

## Phase S01-3: positional decodability

This is reserved and unauthorized. A future contract may freeze one linear

entity, relation, and state. Results must be reported as complete position
maps, including failures. No representation is selected to rescue FAS-00.

does not authorize online readouts, episodic memory, snapshots, recurrent
state, RTRL, JEPA, backbone adapters, or Phase 4 of FAS-00. Any adaptive
experiment requires its own protocol and authorization after S01 findings are
