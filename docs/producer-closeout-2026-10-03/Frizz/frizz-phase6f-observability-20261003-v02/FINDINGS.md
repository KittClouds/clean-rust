## Limits that matter

- Existing bank rows have no conflicting full-observation equivalence classes.
  Their empirical 100% exact-set ceiling reflects unique sampled worlds, not
  proof of identifiability.
- Witnesses affect full legal sets; only 6/333 DEV roots have a changed
  same-selected-type legal set in this first-witness search. **No logged
  selected candidate changes legality.** This does not explain away the
  whole same-type ranking failure or prove the selected-action task impossible.
- Hypothetical original-plus-counterfactual sample ceiling is 70.40% for full
  legal sets; it is **not** an accuracy ceiling for the original bank or Qwen.
- The bounded witness search gives a witnessed lower bound on ambiguity,
  not an exhaustive identifiable/ambiguous classification of the remainder.
